隱私權政策 / Privacy Policy

中文

  • 不收集任何個人資料。不需註冊帳號。

English

  • No personal data collected. No account required.
  • Game records stay on your device, never uploaded; uninstalling deletes everything.
  • Sharing happens only when you tap share, via the system share sheet.
  • Ads: if a future version adds rewarded ads (Google AdMob), they are strictly opt-in (watched in exchange for a hint). AdMob may collect advertising identifiers per its policy — see Google Privacy Policy. This policy will be updated when ads ship.
