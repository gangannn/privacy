English

  • No personal data collected. No account; no ads, analytics, or crash reporting. We have no servers and cannot see any of your data.
  • Your data stays on your phone. Business info, client list, documents and their PDFs, payment records, your saved signature and stamp images, and saved items are stored only on your device and are never uploaded; uninstalling deletes them (files you have saved or shared elsewhere are not affected). Android cloud auto-backup is turned off for this app, so this data is not copied to Google Drive.
  • Photos and camera: Photos you take or pick from your gallery (job-site photos, document scanning, image-to-text, ID watermarking, stamp images) are processed only on your phone; job-site photos are compressed and placed inside that document's PDF. The app gets photos through the system camera or gallery and does not request the camera permission.
  • Signatures: A client's on-site signature is written only into the PDF generated at that moment; it is not stored separately or carried over to other documents. Your own saved signature and stamp are stored on your phone.
  • Document tools: PDFs, images, and Word templates you select are processed offline on your phone (including scanning and text recognition) and are never sent to any server.
  • Sharing and saving: PDFs, payment reminders, and backup files leave the app only when you tap share or save — through the system share sheet to whoever you choose, or into your phone's Documents folder. A full backup is a single file you store wherever you like; it contains all of your data, so keep it safe.
  • Purchases: Pro is available as a monthly/yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase, both billed through Google Play. This app never handles payment data; it only checks your purchase status with Google Play on your phone, which is the only reason it uses the network. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Changes: If this policy changes, this page will be updated along with the date above.
